## Submission materials visible without login

The [submission page](https://hackathons.solana.com/hackathons/stocklana/submit) shows a sign-in prompt and wallet connection control. The authenticated form, exact fields, length limits, validation rules, and consent terms were **not verified**.

The general [How It Works page](https://hackathons.solana.com/how-it-works) lists these optional materials:

- GitHub repository containing open-source code.
- Live demo on devnet or mainnet.
- Pitch video, maximum three minutes.
- Technical walkthrough video, described as five minutes.
- Teammates invited by username.
- Up to three sponsor tracks.

That page says late entries are not accepted. Submissions remain private during judging; winners are displayed afterward, while other teams can choose to publish.

**Apply Stocklana's specific rules when pages differ.** General guidance describes two weeks and optional links; Stocklana describes one week and requires at least one supporting link. [General guidance](https://hackathons.solana.com/how-it-works), [event rules](https://hackathons.solana.com/hackathons/stocklana).

## Implications for the product audit

These are recommendations, not additional organizer rules:

1. Select one submission and one stock-related user flow. Present Wallie, Wallie Cloud, and AllowanceKit as supporting parts only where the inspected implementation supports that claim.
2. Record pre-existing components, their licenses, and dated changes for this event. Do not describe old work as newly built.
3. Demonstrate a tokenized-stock action end to end. General wallet or agent-payment functionality alone does not establish the event fit.
4. Check the authenticated form and its terms before claiming submission readiness. Preserve the repository, demo, and video links as reviewable materials.

This note establishes requirements only. It does not verify the three products' functionality, registration status, eligibility, or submission status.
